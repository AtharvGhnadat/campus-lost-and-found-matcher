Algorithmic Match Finder

The system compares all Lost Reports against Found Reports and calculates how likely they are to be the same item.

⚠️
Clear Verification Notice: A high match percentage means the details look very similar, not proof that the item is yours. You must still verify secret details with campus security or the finder before physical collection.

How the 100-Point Score is Calculated:

1. Same Category (35 pts)
E.g. Wallet vs Wallet
2. Words Overlap (30 pts)
Matching brand & colors
3. Location (20 pts)
Same campus building/zone
4. Date Proximity (15 pts)
Lost & found on similar day
